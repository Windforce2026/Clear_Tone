Hearing loss means reduced ability to detect sounds. It can be temporary or permanent and may affect one ear or both. Our hearing tests help identify the cause and best next steps.
Hearing aids pick up sound, amplify specific frequencies based on your hearing profile, and deliver clearer speech and environmental sounds. Programming ensures comfort and clarity for your daily listening.
We support CGHS facility guidance for eligible patients. Our team helps you understand the steps and prepares the process so you can move forward confidently.
Duration varies by individual goals and progress. Some patients notice changes in weeks, while others benefit from longer structured programs. We create a realistic timeline after assessment.

The best brand depends on your hearing profile, lifestyle, and preferences. Rechargeable hearing aids offer convenience, no battery replacements, and consistent power throughout the day. Battery-powered aids can be useful for those who prefer disposables or need extended usage away from charging. Both options are available and we can recommend based on your routine.
Hearing aid prices vary based on technology level, features, brand, and style. Premium devices with AI, Bluetooth, and rechargeable options are available across budgets. During your consultation, we present clear options aligned with your needs — no hidden pricing or pressure.
With proper care and maintenance, hearing aids typically last between 4 to 7 years. Regular servicing, cleaning, and software updates help extend their lifespan. We offer repair and maintenance support to keep your devices performing at their best.
