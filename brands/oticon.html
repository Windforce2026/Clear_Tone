Brand Story

A legacy of innovation in hearing science

Oticon has been at the forefront of hearing technology for over a century. Their commitment to understanding how the brain processes sound has led to breakthrough innovations that help people hear more naturally in complex listening environments.

With a patient-centered philosophy, Oticon focuses on delivering sound that supports speech understanding, reduces listening effort, and improves memory recall so users can stay engaged in conversations throughout the day.

Key Milestones

  • Pioneering BrainHearing philosophy
  • First to introduce open sound experience
  • Leading-edge connectivity solutions
  • Deep neural network sound processing
Technology

BrainHearing innovations

Oticon's technology works with your brain, not against it — delivering rich, balanced sound that feels natural.
