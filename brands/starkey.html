Brand Story

American innovation with a human touch

Starkey is an American hearing aid manufacturer with a rich history of innovation spanning over 50 years. They are known for pushing the boundaries of what hearing aids can do — transforming them from simple amplification devices into sophisticated wearable health computers.

With a strong commitment to research and a patient-first philosophy, Starkey has pioneered features like fall detection, language translation, and activity tracking integrated directly into hearing aids.

Key Milestones

  • First hearing aid with AI and sensors
  • Integrated fall detection and alerts
  • Health tracking via hearing aids
  • Language translation capabilities
